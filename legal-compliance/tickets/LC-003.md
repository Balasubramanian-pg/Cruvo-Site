# Ticket LC-003: Data Principal Rights & Consent Withdrawal Protocol

**Status**: IN PROGRESS  
**Severity**: CRITICAL  
**Primary Statute**: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (Section 6(4) & Sections 11-14)  
**Regulatory Body**: Data Protection Board of India

## 1. Legal Risk Analysis
Section 6(4) of the DPDP Act 2023 requires that a Data Principal has the right to withdraw consent with the same ease with which it was given. Sections 11 through 14 give citizens:
1. Right to access information about personal data.
2. Right to correction and erasure of personal data.
3. Right of grievance redressal.
4. Right to nominate another individual in the event of death or incapacity.

Failure to provide an actionable channel to withdraw consent or erase client data violates statutory mandates.

## 2. Remediation Requirements
1. Clearly document the exact email procedure (`privacy@cruvo.in` / `cruvoconsulting@gmail.com`) for submitting data access and deletion requests.
2. Guarantee processing of erasure and consent revocation requests within 72 hours.
3. Include explicit rights descriptions in the Privacy Policy modal.
