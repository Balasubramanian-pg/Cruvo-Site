# Ticket LC-013: Intellectual Property & Proprietary Frameworks Protection

**Status**: IN PROGRESS  
**Severity**: MEDIUM  
**Primary Statute**: Copyright Act, 1957 & Trade Marks Act, 1999  
**Regulatory Body**: Intellectual Property India (CGPDTM)

## 1. Legal Risk Analysis
Cruvo develops proprietary financial frameworks, MIS structure definitions, profitability templates, turnaround roadmaps, and website design content.
Without explicit reservation of copyright and trademark rights:
- Competitors or third-party web scrapers can replicate content or claim fair dealing.
- The business loses clear legal grounds to issue Cease & Desist notices or take down infringing material.

## 2. Remediation Requirements
1. Add explicit statutory Copyright notice: `© 2025 Cruvo Consulting. All rights reserved.`
2. Define Cruvo trademarks and service marks in the Terms of Service.
3. Explicitly prohibit automated data extraction, AI scraping, content mirroring, and unauthorized derivative works.
4. Establish an IP infringement notice and takedown contact protocol.

## 3. Implementation Status
- Embedded in Terms of Service (`src/data/legalContent.ts`).
