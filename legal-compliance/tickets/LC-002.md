# Ticket LC-002: Contact Form Free & Unambiguous Consent Checkbox

**Status**: IN PROGRESS  
**Severity**: CRITICAL  
**Primary Statute**: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (Section 6(1))  
**Regulatory Body**: Data Protection Board of India

## 1. Legal Risk Analysis
Section 6(1) of the DPDP Act mandates that consent must be:
- **Free**: Not coerced or bundled unreasonably.
- **Specific**: Tied to clear advisory communication purposes.
- **Informed**: Direct reference and link to the Privacy Policy.
- **Unconditional**: Separated from mere website browsing.
- **Unambiguous**: Accompanied by a clear affirmative action (unticked checkbox).

Submitting user information without affirmative consent is deemed invalid consent under Indian law, opening the firm to regulatory investigation and complaints before the Data Protection Board.

## 2. Remediation Requirements
1. Add an unchecked consent checkbox to `Contact.tsx`.
2. Clearly formulate consent text: *"I consent to Cruvo Consulting processing my contact and business details to respond to my strategic inquiry as detailed in the Privacy Policy."*
3. Form submission MUST be blocked if the checkbox is unticked.
4. Provide immediate visual feedback for validation.
