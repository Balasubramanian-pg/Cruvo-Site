# Ticket LC-009: Exclusive Indian Dispute Resolution & Jurisdiction

**Status**: IN PROGRESS  
**Severity**: HIGH  
**Primary Statute**: Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 & Code of Civil Procedure, 1908  
**Regulatory Body**: Courts of Law (India)

## 1. Legal Risk Analysis
In the absence of an explicit governing law and forum-selection clause:
- A disgruntled client, contractor, or visitor from another Indian state or foreign jurisdiction can file litigation in remote courts, requiring expensive out-of-station litigation and legal representation.
- Under Indian contract law, parties can agree to exclusive territorial jurisdiction among competent courts (e.g. Pune/Mumbai, Maharashtra).

## 2. Remediation Requirements
1. Insert unambiguous Governing Law & Dispute Resolution clause:
   - Governing law: Laws of the Republic of India.
   - Amicable resolution period: Mandatory 30-day good faith discussion.
   - Arbitration: Sole arbitrator under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 seated in Pune/Mumbai, Maharashtra.
   - Exclusive judicial jurisdiction: Courts in Pune/Mumbai, Maharashtra, India.
