# Ticket LC-014: Professional Retainer, Refund & Cancellation Terms

**Status**: IN PROGRESS  
**Severity**: HIGH  
**Primary Statute**: Consumer Protection Act, 2019 & Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020  
**Regulatory Body**: Central Consumer Protection Authority / National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission

## 1. Legal Risk Analysis
SMEs engaging fractional CFOs or booking strategic consultations frequently dispute billing or cancel mid-sprint. Under Indian consumer protection law, failure to publish transparent refund, cancellation, and retainer billing terms constitutes an "unfair trade practice" (Section 2(47) of CPA 2019), which can result in orders for full restitution plus punitive damages in consumer courts.

## 2. Remediation Requirements
1. Clearly disclose in Terms of Service that initial discovery calls or exploratory assessments are subject to scheduled appointment confirmations.
2. Formally outline that retained advisory engagements are governed by individual signed Statements of Work (SOW).
3. Specify that retainer fees are milestone/time-based and non-refundable once professional hours have been dedicated.
4. Provide a standard 14-day written notice requirement for mutual termination.
