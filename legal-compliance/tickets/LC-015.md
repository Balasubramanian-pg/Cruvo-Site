# Ticket LC-015: Financial Information Confidentiality & NDA Safeguard

**Status**: IN PROGRESS  
**Severity**: CRITICAL  
**Primary Statute**: Information Technology Act, 2000 (Section 72A & Section 43A)  
**Regulatory Body**: Cyber Appellate Tribunal / Indian Adjudicating Officers

## 1. Legal Risk Analysis
Indian SME founders submitting details through the website contact form often disclose proprietary financial bottlenecks, revenue figures, cash crunches, or supplier distress.
Under Section 72A of the IT Act, disclosure of personal or business information without consent in breach of lawful contract carries imprisonment up to 3 years or fine up to ₹5 Lakhs.
Furthermore, if such confidential financial disclosures leak or are mishandled, the founder could sue for trade secret violation and breach of fiduciary confidence.

## 2. Remediation Requirements
1. Add an explicit Confidentiality Guarantee on the contact form itself:
   - *"Confidentiality Guaranteed: All financial data and business information submitted are held in strict confidence under non-disclosure principles and will never be shared, leased, or sold."*
2. Integrate an explicit Non-Disclosure clause in the Terms of Service.
3. Establish encryption and secure handling commitments in the Privacy Policy.
