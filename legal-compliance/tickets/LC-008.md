# Ticket LC-008: Enforceable Terms of Use / Terms of Service

**Status**: IN PROGRESS  
**Severity**: CRITICAL  
**Primary Statute**: Indian Contract Act, 1872 & Information Technology Act, 2000  
**Regulatory Body**: Courts of Law (India)

## 1. Legal Risk Analysis
Operating a commercial website without an enforceable Terms of Use agreement leaves the business vulnerable to unbounded claims:
- No contractual definitions of user obligations.
- No disclaimer of warranties on content ("as is", "as available").
- No rights reserved to revoke access or terminate malicious visits.
- Unprotected proprietary methodologies and MIS frameworks.

Under Indian contract law, browsewrap and clickwrap agreements require reasonable notice and accessibility to bind visitors.

## 2. Remediation Requirements
1. Draft comprehensive Terms of Service covering:
   - Scope of website access and intellectual property.
   - User code of conduct (no scraping, reverse engineering).
   - "As Is" information disclaimer.
   - Third-party link disclaimers.
   - Right to amend terms.
2. Link Terms of Service in the footer and contact submission form.
