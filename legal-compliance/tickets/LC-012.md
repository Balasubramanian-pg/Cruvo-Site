# Ticket LC-012: Cookie & Tracking Notice with User Choice Banner

**Status**: IN PROGRESS  
**Severity**: MEDIUM  
**Primary Statute**: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 & Information Technology Act, 2000  
**Regulatory Body**: Data Protection Board of India

## 1. Legal Risk Analysis
While India does not enforce the EU ePrivacy Directive, the DPDP Act 2023 defines digital personal data processing broadly to include IP addresses, persistent identifiers, and behavioural analytics.
Processing telemetry and analytics cookies without giving prior notification and opt-out/accept controls impairs the "informed" consent requirement under Section 6 of the DPDP Act.

## 2. Remediation Requirements
1. Build a non-intrusive Cookie Consent Banner (`CookieConsentBanner.tsx`).
2. Clearly explain usage: essential cookies (required for basic security/navigation) and analytical cookies.
3. Provide "Accept All", "Reject Non-Essential", and link to the Privacy Policy.
4. Persist choice locally in `localStorage` without re-prompting unnecessarily.
