# Ticket LC-006: SEBI Non-Investment Advisory Statutory Disclaimer

**Status**: IN PROGRESS  
**Severity**: CRITICAL  
**Primary Statute**: SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013 & SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014  
**Regulatory Body**: Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)

## 1. Legal Risk Analysis
In India, unauthorized financial advisory or recommendations related to securities, capital raising, or investment returns falls strictly under SEBI purview. Under Section 12(1) of the SEBI Act, 1992, unregistered investment advice carries penalties of up to ₹25 Crores or three times the amount of profits made, along with criminal liability.

Because Cruvo uses terms like *"Strategic Finance"*, *"Capital Unlocked"*, *"Enterprise Value"*, and *"Cost of Capital"*, it could be construed by MSMEs as providing capital market, securities, or SEBI-regulated advice.

## 2. Remediation Requirements
1. Add explicit statutory disclaimer:
   - *"Cruvo Consulting is not a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser (RIA), Research Analyst (RA), or Portfolio Manager. All services are strictly confined to internal operational business finance, MIS design, working capital tracking, and managerial accounting."*
   - *"Nothing on this website constitutes financial investment advice, securities recommendations, or an offer to solicit investments."*
