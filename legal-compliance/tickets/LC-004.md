# Ticket LC-004: Statutory Grievance Redressal Officer Publication

**Status**: IN PROGRESS  
**Severity**: CRITICAL  
**Primary Statute**: IT (Reasonable Security Practices) Rules, 2011 (Rule 5(9)) & IT Rules, 2021 (Rule 3(2))  
**Regulatory Body**: Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)

## 1. Legal Risk Analysis
Indian cyber law mandates every body corporate collecting user data to designate a Grievance Officer and publish their:
- Full Name
- Designation
- Official physical address
- Direct email address and contact telephone number
- Mechanism for receiving complaints

Failure to do so violates Section 43A of the IT Act 2000, stripping statutory safe harbor protections and exposing the firm to civil compensation claims in case of any data dispute.

## 2. Remediation Requirements
1. Designate a Grievance Officer (e.g., Compliance Officer / Anika Nair).
2. Explicitly publish contact details in the website footer and Privacy Policy.
3. State standard Indian resolution commitments: Acknowledgment within 24-36 hours and full resolution within 15 days.
