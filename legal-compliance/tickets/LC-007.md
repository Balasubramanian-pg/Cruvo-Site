# Ticket LC-007: Performance Metrics & Turnaround Claims Disclaimers

**Status**: IN PROGRESS  
**Severity**: HIGH  
**Primary Statute**: Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (Section 2(28)) & CCPA Guidelines for Prevention of Misleading Advertisements, 2022  
**Regulatory Body**: Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA)

## 1. Legal Risk Analysis
Section 2(28) of the Consumer Protection Act defines misleading advertisement as any claim that gives a false guarantee or is likely to mislead consumers regarding nature, substance, quantity, or quality.
The CCPA Guidelines 2022 mandate that:
1. All quantitative assertions (e.g., *"₹1.5Cr+ Capital Unlocked"*, *"25% Margin Improvement"*, *"15% CAC Reduction"*) must be substantiated by empirical records.
2. Disclaimers must be prominent, visible, and legible, clarifying that individual results vary depending on client implementation, market conditions, and baseline records.

Failing to qualify these claims exposes the directors/founder to CCPA penalties up to ₹10 Lakhs for the first violation and up to ₹50 Lakhs for subsequent violations.

## 2. Remediation Requirements
1. Add footnote indicators (`*`) to the Hero subtitle, Stats numbers, and Case Study section.
2. Append a clear statutory qualification disclaimer:
   - *"Metrics cited are based on specific historical client engagements and do not constitute a guarantee or projection of future performance. Actual outcomes depend upon client execution, industry sector, and management diligence."*
