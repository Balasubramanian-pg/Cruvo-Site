# Ticket LC-001: Implement DPDP Act 2023 Compliant Privacy Policy

**Status**: IN PROGRESS  
**Severity**: CRITICAL  
**Primary Statute**: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (Section 5 & 6)  
**Regulatory Body**: Data Protection Board of India

## 1. Legal Risk Analysis
Without a comprehensive Privacy Policy complying with the DPDP Act 2023:
- Collecting business owners' names, email addresses, phone numbers, and company financial pain points without prior statutory notice exposes the firm to penalties up to ₹250 Crores under Schedule 1 of the DPDP Act 2023 for failure to take reasonable security safeguards or give adequate notice.
- The policy must clearly specify:
  1. What personal data is processed.
  2. The specific purpose of processing.
  3. The manner in which Data Principals may exercise their rights (access, correction, erasure, grievance).
  4. The contact details of the Grievance Officer.

## 2. Remediation Requirements
1. Author a full Indian DPDP Act 2023 and IT SPDI Rules 2011 compliant Privacy Policy.
2. Outline specific purposes: client communication, financial diagnostics scheduling, contractual fulfillment, and statutory compliance.
3. Explicitly state data retention periods and deletion upon request.
4. Provide direct access link in the website footer and contact submission form.
