# Ticket LC-005: ICAI Advertising & Non-Solicitation Ethics Disclaimer

**Status**: IN PROGRESS  
**Severity**: CRITICAL  
**Primary Statute**: Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 (First Schedule, Part I, Clauses 6 & 7)  
**Regulatory Body**: Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI)

## 1. Legal Risk Analysis
Under Clause 6 and Clause 7 of Part I of the First Schedule to the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949, a Chartered Accountant in practice is deemed guilty of professional misconduct if they:
1. Solicit clients or professional work either directly or indirectly.
2. Advertise professional attainments or services, or make claims of superiority.

The website identifies founder **Anika Nair** as a *"Chartered Accountant"*, and lists commercial metrics like *"25% Margin Improvement"*, *"₹1.5Cr Capital Unlocked"*. If Cruvo is perceived as a practicing CA firm soliciting audit/tax clients, ICAI can initiate disciplinary proceedings against the member, including suspension or cancellation of Certificate of Practice (COP).

## 2. Remediation Requirements
1. Insert prominent regulatory disclaimer clarifying corporate scope:
   - *"Cruvo Consulting is a strategic management and business consulting firm. Cruvo does not provide statutory audit, attest services, or any professional services reserved exclusively for practicing Chartered Accountants under the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949."*
2. Add ICAI non-solicitation disclaimer:
   - *"This website is intended solely for informational purposes regarding management advisory services and does not constitute an advertisement, personal communication, or solicitation of professional accounting or auditing services."*
