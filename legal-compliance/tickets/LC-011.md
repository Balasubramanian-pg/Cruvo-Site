# Ticket LC-011: Entity Legal Identification & Registered Office Disclosure

**Status**: IN PROGRESS  
**Severity**: HIGH  
**Primary Statute**: Companies Act, 2013 (Section 12(3)(c)) / Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008 / Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020  
**Regulatory Body**: Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) / Consumer Affairs

## 1. Legal Risk Analysis
Operating an anonymous or incompletely disclosed commercial website violates Indian business disclosure laws:
- Section 12(3)(c) of the Companies Act requires companies to print their registered name, registered office address, Corporate Identity Number (CIN), email, and phone on websites and official notices.
- Consumer Protection rules mandate clear disclosure of geographic location, legal name of entity, and customer service contacts to prevent deceptive trade practices.

## 2. Remediation Requirements
1. Clearly disclose the registered entity name (e.g. *Cruvo Consulting Advisory Services* / Sole Proprietorship / LLP).
2. Display the registered office address in Maharashtra, India.
3. Provide official contact email and operational phone numbers.
