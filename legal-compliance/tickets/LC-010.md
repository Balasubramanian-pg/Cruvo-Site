# Ticket LC-010: Consequential Damages Exclusion & Liability Cap

**Status**: IN PROGRESS  
**Severity**: CRITICAL  
**Primary Statute**: Indian Contract Act, 1872 (Section 73)  
**Regulatory Body**: Courts of Law (India)

## 1. Legal Risk Analysis
Consulting on working capital, procurement, pricing, and financial controls carries significant business risk. If a client mismanages cash based loosely on a discussion or MIS model and faces insolvency or revenue drops, they may attempt to sue Cruvo for millions in lost profits and consequential damages under Section 73 of the Indian Contract Act.

Without an ironclad limitation of liability clause:
- Liability is uncapped.
- Cruvo is exposed to indirect and speculative damages.

## 2. Remediation Requirements
1. Insert explicit exclusion of all indirect, special, incidental, punitive, or consequential damages (including loss of profits, business disruption, or goodwill).
2. Insert absolute monetary cap: Cruvo's aggregate liability under any cause of action shall strictly not exceed the amount actually paid by the client in the 3 months preceding the claim or ₹10,000 (for general website users).
